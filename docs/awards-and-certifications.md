### Aspire Leaders Program
Edwin Freedman describes leadership as the ability to define a clear and inspiring vision for others.
Before the Aspire Leaders Program by Aspire Institute , I saw leadership as a formal title. Now, I understand it’s a personal quality that starts with me, regardless of my position.
I am grateful for the chance to unlearn my old views. Completing Module 2 and a project proposal has given me practical skills that I am confident will improve my daily interactions with peers and superiors in any future work environment.
