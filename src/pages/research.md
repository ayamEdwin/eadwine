## Research Interest (Statement)

Legged locomotion, safety, and interaction between humans and robots are the intersections of my research interests. While modern robots can increasingly track trajectories and recover from disturbance in controlled settings, the central challenge is not simply getting a robot to walk. When operating in uncertain, human-populated environments, it is creating a safe, resilient, and socially aware system. According to [Kai Williams](https://www.understandingai.org/p/why-humanoid-robots-wont-catch-up#footnote-anchor-3) at [Understanding AI](https://www.understandingai.org/p/), the biggest engineering challenge for humanoid robots is safety. Most current legged humanoids have to actively maintain balance. If, for whatever reason, the motors in the legs stop running, the robot will fall down, potentially injuring people in the process. This shows that real-world failure cases of humanoid robots reveal a crucial gap: locomotion is not only a control problem, but also a safety and interaction problem in human settings.
 I want to solve the problem of safety in legged humanoid locomotion by combining physically grounded locomotion research with human factors and interaction-aware design. In other words, what happens when robots have to navigate clutter, recover from unanticipated disturbances, and interact with people without causing harm or discomfort. Rather than focusing only on training robots to walk in idealized conditions, I want to study how robots can remain stable, predictable, and trustworthy in the real world, especially when their motion is shaped by humans, uncertainty, and physical constraints.
 My long-term objective is to create legged robots that are not only able to move around, but also move in shared spaces in a safe and context-aware manner. I am motivated by the fact that the most important failures in robotics are often not in simulation or idealized tests, but in moments where a robot slips, falls, collides, or behaves unpredictably around people. That is the problem I want to address.
